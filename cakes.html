How a custom cake order works

Tell us the occasion, the date, and roughly how many people it's for. We'll talk through flavour, size, and design — from a simple single-tier cake to a multi-tier wedding centrepiece.

Standard birthday cakes need at least 48 hours' notice. Wedding cakes and large event orders should be booked 1–2 weeks ahead, especially around December and festive weekends.
