Meet Kissie

Kissie started baking the way most Ghanaian bakers do — in a home kitchen, selling meat pies and cupcakes to neighbours and church members on weekends. Word travelled faster than the trays could come out of the oven.

Today, Kissie's Bakery still runs on that same standard: real butter, proper proofing time, and nothing pulled out early to meet a deadline. Every pie, loaf and cake is made to be picked up warm, not just fresh.

— Kissie
